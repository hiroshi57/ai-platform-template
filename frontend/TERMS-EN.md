## 1. This tool is not a certification body

The "score" and "recommended verdict" calculated by this tool are reference
information based on content entered by the recorder. They are **not**
official verification or certification by the Japan Fact-check Center (JFC),
IFCN member organizations, or any other authority.

## 2. The user is responsible for the final decision to publish

If you use this tool's output to publish or distribute statements about the
truth or falsity of claims concerning third parties (politicians, public
figures, organizations, etc.), **you personally bear legal responsibility**
for matters such as defamation or damage to credit. Before publishing, a
human must always confirm that the evidence is sufficient and that facts are
clearly separated from opinion/speculation.

## 3. Avoid definitive conclusions without sufficient evidence

Publishing a definitive verdict such as "false" or "true" when none of
Source Verification, Primary Source Comparison, or Cross-Checking has any
recorded evidence may be treated as an unsubstantiated assertion and can
increase the risk of defamation or similar claims.

## 4. Handling of data

Entered content is, in principle, stored only on your device (in the
browser's localStorage). Only if you actively use the "Server Share" feature
will the shared content be stored on the provider's server, at which point
any third party who knows the link can view or edit it.

## 5. Handling of third-party copyrighted material

If you paste third-party copyrighted material (e.g., full-text news articles)
into the "body / transcript" field, please ensure this satisfies the
requirements for lawful quotation under copyright law (minimum necessary
extent, clear attribution, etc.).

## 6. Disclaimer

The provider is not liable for any damages incurred by the user or any third
party arising from use of this tool, including publication or provision to
third parties of scores or verdicts calculated by the tool.

## 7. Relationship to the general Terms of Service / Privacy Policy

The Terms of Service and Privacy Policy linked from the footer are a shared
template used across sibling tools, and do not individually address risks
specific to fact-checking work. Before selling this tool commercially to
press organizations or corporate customers, you must have your own legal
counsel review the contract terms.
